I am a computational scientist with a Ph.D. in Astrochemistry (Université Paris-Saclay) and more than three years as a postdoctoral scientist in marine ecosystem modelling at the University of Hamburg (with parental leave in 2024). There I built a Lagrangian individual-based model of phytoplankton trait evolution, worked in the GOTM-FABM water-column framework, engineered simulations in Fortran, analysed large NetCDF datasets in Python, and managed multi-year hindcast and projection experiments on HPC clusters. In 2025 I continued this work as a guest scientist at Helmholtz-Zentrum Hereon in Geesthacht.

I then spent a year in clinical data engineering, which sharpened a different discipline: verification. Refactoring a production R pipeline that processes 143,000+ patient records annually from ~300 German clinics, I reduced it by 26.5% while proving byte-identical output at every step, and escalated two pre-existing bugs for supervisor review rather than silently fixing them. I also built an interactive Shiny dashboard for clinical quality metrics, available as a live demo.

My current independent research analyses what a transformer computes internally, and there I found and publicly corrected two silent errors in my own analysis pipeline. The same question runs through all of this work: what is the model actually computing, and how far can its output be trusted? I am looking for my next role anywhere in Germany — scientific computing, data engineering, machine learning or research software. I hold German work authorisation and am available immediately.
